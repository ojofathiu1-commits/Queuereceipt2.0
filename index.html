THE PROBLEM WITH PAPER RECEIPTS

Easy to lose.
Hard to find.

Every receipt is proof of a purchase — for a return, a warranty claim, an expense report, or a tax deduction. Too often it is lost or unreadable by the time you need it. QueueReceipts keeps every one secure, organised, and ready.

🧾

Ink fades

Thermal receipts often become unreadable within months — exactly when you need proof for a warranty or return.

🔍

Records get scattered

Proof of purchase ends up spread across email, drawers, and different apps, making it hard to find when it matters.

💸

Money slips away

Forgotten returns, missed warranty claims, and untracked spending can quietly add up to hundreds of pounds each year.

HOW IT WORKS

Two ways in.
One organised record.

However a receipt reaches you — a retailer's QR code or a paper slip — QueueReceipts captures it and takes care of the rest. No folders to create, no spreadsheets to maintain.

1

Capture it

At participating retailers, a QR code appears after you pay — scan it and the full receipt is sent to the app. Anywhere else, photograph a paper receipt or upload a digital one.

2

We extract the details

QueueReceipts reads the merchant, items, totals, taxes, and date, then files everything in the correct category automatically.

3

Find it anytime

Search by merchant, item, or date to retrieve any receipt in seconds, with spending totals already calculated.
